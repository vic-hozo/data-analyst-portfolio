# IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dashboard

## Project Overview

An Excel-based HR analytics project analyzing employee attrition and workforce data. The dashboard provides insights into employee turnover, departments, job roles, compensation, overtime, and other workforce factors.

## Skills

* Data Cleaning
* Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
* XLOOKUP
* SUMIFS & COUNTIFS
* UNIQUE, SORT & FILTER
* Dashboard Development
* KPI Reporting
* Data Visualization

## Key Analysis

* Employee attrition and retention
* Attrition by department and job role
* Monthly income and compensation
* Overtime and attrition
* Employee job levels and years at company
